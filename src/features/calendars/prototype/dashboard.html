How the day reshuffles. The plan follows the real clock (clinic time). Urgent and High tasks take the first hour. Normal and Low tasks wait in the hours after it and are marked "if she has time". Walk-ins can arrive at any hour, so each hour keeps about 30 minutes free. Once every Urgent and High task is ticked, she is ahead: the buffer lifts and Normal and Low tasks move up into what is left of the hour, one by one as far as they fit, and each tick pulls the next one up. Let an hour pass and whatever is left moves to the next hour with room, still in priority order. Anything that won't fit before close rolls to the next open day. Tasks skip front-desk blocks (lunch 14:00 to 14:45, a patient arriving, check-in around each booking). Bookings are fixed and never move. Final pass. Before opening, a second layer re-reads the call log, corrects Jade's mistakes and writes the suggestions on the task cards. Task times average about 3 minutes a call and are working estimates (see CATS in the file), not benchmarked averages.
