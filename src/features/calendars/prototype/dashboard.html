How the day reshuffles. Walk-ins can arrive at any hour, so each hour gets about 30 minutes of tasks (the mark on each hour's bar), highest priority first. Urgent tasks can use the whole hour and High tasks can stretch to 40 minutes, so they are never held back. Tick a task and its time is freed, so later tasks are pulled up. Miss the hour and whatever is left is pushed to the next hour with room. Anything that won't fit before close rolls to the next open day. Bookings are fixed and never move. Task times average about 3 minutes a call and are working estimates (see CATS in the file), not benchmarked averages.
